## Role overview (verbatim)

As Principal Program Manager, Go-To-Market, you own two equal pillars of go-to-market execution for Product & Technical Marketing (PTM). First, you are the strategic and hands-on architect behind GitLab's high-stakes minor and major launches, redesigning the launch operating model with AI agents at its core so customers discover, adopt, and experience GitLab with precision. 

 Second, you run program management on key initiatives that span Product Management and Product Design through Growth Marketing, Comms and Social, and Field Enablement, wherever those motions depend on PTM. PTM team is responsible for product and solutions marketing, technical writing and documentation, developer advocacy, and globalization, and acts as the glue between these functions across a variety of go-to-market initiatives.

 You are AI-first by default. When programs still run on many manual steps, you convert that work into team-wide AI workflows, either by shipping the automations yourself or by partnering with the AI Transformation Owner (ATO) for the marketing organization to drive shared skills, adoption, and governance. You are not a reactive deadline chaser. You drive solutions with stakeholders as dates approach, design decision stages so tradeoffs land early, and redesign stages and workflows when product roadmap, growth priorities, or major launch decisions change. You create clarity in ambiguity: frame the problem, present options and tradeoffs, name owners, and optimize for decision velocity.

 In your first year, you institutionalize a dual operating system: a scalable launch framework for 15+ minor and major launches per year, and a portfolio program practice that maps dependencies from Product and Design through PTM into Growth, Comms, and Field. You ship production agent workflows that cut manual coordination, establish benchmarks for quality, and make go-to-market program management achieve relevance in the AI era, and become a trusted partner across the organization. 

 Reporting to the VP of Product & Technical Marketing, you operate with significant executive visibility and autonomy. You measure success by what lands for customers and the business: pipeline where it matters, and brand and community impact where business requires it, not by activity volume alone.

 Some examples of projects: 

 
- Orchestrate minor launches that put new platform releases in front of the community

- Orchestrate major launches tied to company events that move pipeline

- Orchestrate weekly signal announcements so we stay present in a fast-moving ecosystem

- Sequence product decisions into PTM and other go-to-market functions, with clear handoffs

- Keep field enablement on track when they depend on messaging and sales assets

- Design decision points early so blockers show up before the deadline

- Scope launches for pipeline and for post-launch adoption when agentic products are involved

- Build AI workflows that draft briefs, synthesize status, and cut manual program steps

- Rebuild stages and calendars when roadmap, growth priorities, or launch plans change

- Run retrospectives that improve the system, and remove process as often as you add it
