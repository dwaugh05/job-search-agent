## What this job asks you to do (verbatim)

- Own the full operating lifecycle of the marketing organization’s AI agents and automated workflows, including provisioning, deployment, monitoring, output verification, alerting, and incident response.

- Design, build, test, and continuously optimize agentic workflows and automations that eliminate repetitive manual processes and improve marketing team productivity.

- Evaluate marketing processes and apply sound business judgment to determine which workflows should be automated and where automation will create the greatest impact.

- Instrument automation and AI systems to track usage, output quality, performance, and business value, using feedback to continuously improve their effectiveness.

- Establish consistent definitions for core marketing concepts such as territories, lead-scoring stages, MQLs, MQAs, pipeline stages, and targets across tools, dashboards, agents, and product lines.

- Own the marketing technology stack, including configuration, administration, integrations, and ongoing improvements, while enabling campaign teams to handle routine changes independently.

- Build and maintain production-grade integrations and automations using platforms such as Clay, Zapier, Workato, n8n, or comparable technologies.

- Develop reliable data and workflow infrastructure that can operate unattended, with appropriate monitoring and safeguards to identify failures before they impact business operations.

- Collaborate closely with marketing operations and broader go-to-market stakeholders to translate business requirements into scalable technical solutions.

- Improve engineering practices within marketing technology by introducing version control, code review, testing, CI/CD, shared repositories, and maintainable development processes.

- Help advance the use of AI across marketing workflows by identifying opportunities to increase speed, quality, leverage, and operational efficiency.

## Requirements / qualifications (verbatim)

- Previous professional experience in marketing or marketing operations is required, with a strong understanding of marketing funnels, attribution, lead scoring, campaign execution, deliverability, list hygiene, and consent practices.

- Demonstrated experience building and operating production automation within a go-to-market or marketing function, using tools such as Clay, Zapier, Workato, n8n, or similar platforms.

- Experience owning unattended systems, pipelines, integrations, or AI agents, including diagnosing failures, validating outputs, and implementing monitoring and safeguards.

- Evidence of progressing from no-code or low-code automation into software engineering practices, including version control, code reviews, testing, CI, and deployment through shared repositories.

- Working proficiency in SQL and Python , with solid REST API fundamentals and an understanding of secure secrets and credential management.

- Familiarity with data warehouses such as BigQuery and the ability to query, interpret, and reason about data models.

- Hands-on experience administering and integrating HubSpot and Salesforce .

- Strong understanding of how marketing technology, data, automation, and AI systems interact within a broader GTM ecosystem.

- Strong analytical and troubleshooting abilities, with a mindset focused on verifying system outputs rather than relying solely on successful execution statuses.

- Experience with Claude or n8n is a plus.

- Experience developing multi-step enrichment workflows in Clay or comparable platforms is advantageous.

- Exposure to observability and data-quality practices, including monitoring, alerting, data freshness, and anomaly detection, is highly valued.

- Experience supporting multiple product lines or business motions where data and metrics must remain consistent across systems is a plus.

- Strong ownership, judgment, communication, adaptability, and comfort working in an environment where priorities can change quickly.

- Demonstrated AI fluency and the ability to use AI tools to accelerate workflows, structure problems, improve quality, and increase technical leverage.
