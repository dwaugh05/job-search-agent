## What this job asks you to do (verbatim)

Let’s do this. Let’s change the world. The Director of Marketing AI Capabilities, within the Global Customer Capabilities & Innovation organization (GCCI), will be responsible for leading the development and scaling of AI-enabled marketing capabilities, operating models, and ways of working across Amgen. The Director will serve as a strategic connector between emerging external trends and internal enterprise transformation — translating advances in AI technologies, evolving customer expectations, new ecosystem partnerships, and changing marketing practices into scalable capabilities that drive meaningful business impact.
 
 Responsibilities include: 
 
 External Landscape & Strategic Foresight 
- Monitor and assess the evolving AI, digital marketing, data, and customer engagement landscape, including emerging technologies, platforms, partners, and competitive trends
- Identify disruptive shifts in customer behaviors, content consumption, channel dynamics, and personalization expectations
- Translate external market developments into actionable marketing capability strategies
 
 Future of Marketing Design, Development and Implementation 
- Work across functions to lead the execution of the Future of Marketing strategy, including the development and implementation of new technology, operating models, and innovative external partnerships
- Lead the design and development of critical technology initiatives, including the Marketing Command Center, and play an advisory role to enabling Neural Nexis initiatives 
- Design future-state marketing operating models, workflows, governance structures, and processes that embed AI into day-to-day execution
- Ensure talent initiatives align with the future marketer vision and AI-enabled capabilities
 
 External Enterprise Partnerships & Innovation Pilot Management 
- Manage existing strategic enterprise partnerships and evaluate new partnerships with AI vendors, platform providers, agencies, consultancies, and emerging technology companies
- Lead pilot programs and proof-of-concept initiatives to assess new capabilities and accelerate innovation
- Build scalable approaches for integrating external solutions into Amgen’s marketing ecosystem
 
 Change Leadership & Organizational Enablement 
- Drive organizational readiness and adoption of AI-enabled ways of working
- Develop enablement strategies, talent models, and capability-building programs to increase AI fluency across marketing teams
- Lead cross-functional teams and influence senior stakeholders across commercial, digital, medical, technology, and operations organizations
